# The data package

The data is distributed through the Kaggle competition Data page, not through
this repository. Download it and point the scripts at it with `--release-root`.

## Layout

```text
<release-root>/
  config/
    synthetic_release_v1.json     calendar, scenario contract, Task 2 window policy
    corridors.json                the ten directional corridors
  corridors/<PANEL>/
    network/
      links.csv                   length, lanes, free speed, capacity
      fd_parameters.csv           per-link fundamental-diagram parameters and lane counts
      lwr_mainline_topology.csv   which link feeds which
      ramp_attachment_map.csv     where ramps attach
      path_set.csv                paths, with origin and destination zones
      path_link_incidence.csv     which links each path uses
      base_od.csv                 the weak OD prior
    train/
      mainline_states/year_month=.../     observations, no Task 1 mask
      mainline_states_masked/mask_regime=R1|R2|R3/
                                          the same, with the Task 1 targets blanked
      ramp_states/                        on- and off-ramp flows
    validation/  private/
      mainline_states_masked/mask_regime=R1|R2|R3/
      ramp_states/
  task1/<PANEL>/<split>/sample_submission_state.csv
  task2/<PANEL>/<split>/window_index.csv, window_history.parquet, sample_submission_queue.csv
  task4/<PANEL>/<split>/synthetic_link_counts.csv, synthetic_weak_prior.csv,
                        sample_submission_path_flow.csv
```

The Kaggle download unpacks to a single `kaggle_public/` directory holding
everything above. Point `--release-root` at that directory, not at its parent.

## The three splits

| Split | Dates | Days | Masked inputs | Unmasked observations |
|---|---|---:|:--:|:--:|
| `train` | 2030-06-01 to 2031-02-28 | 273 | yes | **yes** |
| `validation` | 2031-03 | 31 | yes | no |
| `private` | 2031-04 | 30 | yes | no |

`train` is the only split that ships the unmasked observation layer. Recover the
answer for a blanked cell by joining `mainline_states_masked` to
`mainline_states` on `(timestamp, station_id, link_id)`. `score_task1.py` does
exactly this.

Those dates are synthetic and match no real period. Do not read anything into
the year.

`validation` and `private` were generated independently of each other. They are
different months, with their own demand draws and their own incident schedules:
five incidents in validation, seven in private, at different times and places.
Neither is a held-out sample of the other, so a model fitted until it reproduces
validation has learned that month rather than the corridor.

## What you can and cannot score yourself

| Task | Score it locally? | Why |
|---|:--:|---|
| 1 | **yes**, on `train` | the unmasked observations are released |
| 2 | no | queue labels are withheld on every split |
| 3 | no | conservation needs organizer boundary flows, which are the flow field itself |
| 4 | partly | the reference is derived locally, so it checks your solver, not your rank |

Task 3 deserves a word, because `score_task3.py` runs and prints a number even
though that number means nothing on the public package. Without the organizer
boundary flows the evaluator falls back to a topology estimate. That estimate is
too coarse for the check, so `S_LWR` floors at zero for every submission. On
`D12_I5_N` a perfect answer scores 0.3301 locally and the naive baseline scores
0.3299. Develop against Task 1 instead. It is the only lever on `S_physics`, and
it does score.

## The quality rule

Every mainline and ramp cell carries `pct_observed`, the share of the
five-minute interval a detector actually reported.

```text
is_imputed        = pct_observed < 100
is_score_eligible = pct_observed >= 75 and the required values are non-null
```

Only eligible cells are scored, in every task. Roughly 76% of mainline cells and
76% of ramp cells are eligible, uniformly across the ten corridors. Ineligible
cells are still released. They are part of the problem, not a defect.

An unavailable ramp observation is **unavailable evidence, never a measured zero
flow.** The evaluator excludes those transitions rather than assume the ramp was
empty. Treat them as zero and Task 3 will cost you.

## Provenance

The released records are synthetic. They were generated by a calibrated
traffic-flow model. Its parameters were measured from real freeway detector data
on these same corridors: free-flow speed, per-lane capacity, jam density, demand
by weekday and time of day, the size of the measurement noise, and how often
detectors are available. No real detector record appears in the release. Nothing
was shifted, resampled or copied, and the released calendar matches no real
period.

The consequence for you: the data obeys real traffic physics and carries
realistic measurement error, but it cannot be looked up. There is no external
source to join against.
